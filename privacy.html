MouseLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

MouseLens is a local-first macOS screen recording and editing app. It is designed to create polished screen demos without requiring an account or uploading your recordings to a server.

Data Collection

MouseLens does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

MouseLens does not require an account and does not upload your recordings to a server.

Recordings and Project Files

When you record with MouseLens, screen or window video, optional audio, optional camera video, pointer events, captions, project metadata, and exported videos or GIFs are stored locally on your Mac.

You control these files and can delete them from your Mac at any time.

Permissions

MouseLens may request macOS permissions for:

  • Screen Recording: to capture the screen or selected windows.
  • Microphone: to include microphone audio and generate captions when enabled.
  • Camera: to include the presenter camera bubble when enabled.
  • Speech Recognition: to generate editable captions from recorded speech when requested.

These permissions are used only for the app features you choose to use.

Network Use

MouseLens does not use network services for recording, editing, or exporting videos.

Third Parties

MouseLens does not share recordings or project data with third parties.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact guoxinling_xisu@163.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as MouseLens changes. If future versions add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, or other network features, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before release.
