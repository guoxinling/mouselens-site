Contact

For support, contact guoxinling_xisu@163.com.

What to Include

To help diagnose issues quickly, please include:

  • Your macOS version.
  • Your MouseLens version.
  • Whether the issue happened in Screen mode or Window mode.
  • A short description of the recording, editing, caption, cursor, or export problem.
  • Screenshots or exported sample clips if they are safe to share.

About MouseLens

MouseLens is a macOS screen recording and editing app for creating polished screen walkthrough videos with smart zoom, visible cursor movement, click feedback, presenter camera, captions, trimming, backgrounds, padding, rounded corners, MP4 export, and GIF export.

Privacy

MouseLens is local-first. Recordings and project files stay on your Mac unless you choose to share or export them. Read the Privacy Policy.
